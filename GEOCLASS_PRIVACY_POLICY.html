1. Your projects, files and location

Geoclass works with the coordinates, measurements, settings and project files you enter or import. The purchase-verification server does not receive your survey database or imported project files.

File import, export and sharing use the files and destinations you choose. If you share a file with another app, email service or storage provider, that recipient handles it under its own practices. Copies you export or share are separate from Geoclass's local data.

Location permission lets location-based tools show your position and perform related calculations. You can change this permission in Android settings. Denying it can prevent current-position features; it does not stop you entering coordinates manually. Displaying an online map around your position can disclose that area to the map provider.

2. Online maps

Map services receive requests for the area being displayed, together with your IP address and technical connection information. At close zoom levels, a map centred on you can reveal your location quite precisely. A map request does not upload your entire project.

Depending on the map or tool selected, Geoclass uses services from OpenFreeMap, Esri and OpenStreetMap Foundation. Their linked notices explain their processing and retention practices. You can avoid these requests by not using online-map features.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

The ad-supported app uses Google AdMob and Unity Ads mediation. Advertising services process information such as IP-derived approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, device/software details, ad views and interactions, and performance diagnostics. Unity's disclosures also cover purchase history and, depending on configuration, app-usage information. These services use information to deliver and measure advertising, analyse performance and prevent fraud.

Geoclass uses Google's consent tools to present advertising choices where required. You can revisit available choices through Privacy Settings in the app and manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily stop the technical processing involved in non-personalised advertising or security.

Ad-Free stops Geoclass displaying its advertisements. It does not disable online maps, purchase verification or services you choose to use.

More information: Google Privacy Policy, Google's use of information from partner apps and Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Ad-Free purchases and automatic restoration

Ad-Free is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. Google Play processes payment and manages purchase ownership. Geoclass does not receive your card number or security code.

To verify or restore Ad-Free, the app sends a purchase token, a Play Integrity token and a request identifier over HTTPS to our Google Cloud service. Google Play supplies purchase information, such as the product, purchase status, acknowledgement and cancellation/refund state. Play Integrity supplies app-authenticity, licensing and device-integrity information to help reject fraudulent requests.

Our receipt database stores a one-way digest of the purchase token, the app/product identifiers, the verification result and its time. This record is pseudonymous, not anonymous. The receipt document does not store the raw purchase token, raw Integrity token, Google account email, order number or card details. Hosting logs can contain IP/network information, request times, response codes and diagnostics.

The app keeps purchase tokens and verification state in encrypted Android storage, excluded from app backup. After reinstalling, it can restore a valid purchase automatically when you are online and using the purchasing Google Play account. Verified access can remain available offline.

Verification automatically determines whether Ad-Free is active. Confirmed cancellation or refund can remove access after an online check; an offline device cannot learn about a new refund immediately. If the result seems wrong, contact us for a review. Do not buy again merely because verification failed.

8. How long information is kept

  • Local projects and settings: remain on your device until you delete them or the relevant app data. Exported/shared copies must be removed separately.
  • Verification records: have no automatic expiry. We retain the minimal record while needed to support and verify the one-time entitlement or resolve a purchase dispute or fraud issue. We assess deletion requests and whether continued retention remains necessary; a lifetime purchase is not an automatic reason to refuse deletion.
  • Operational logs: the project's global log-storage buckets have retention periods of 30 days and 400 days, depending on the bucket receiving the log. These logs support troubleshooting, security investigation and operational accountability. Relevant records may need to be kept while an incident, dispute or applicable legal requirement remains unresolved.
  • Support messages: are retained while handling the enquiry and any related unresolved issue, dispute or legal obligation. We remove information when these purposes no longer justify keeping it.

Independent providers, including Google Play, have their own retention arrangements. Our deletion process does not delete their independent payment records.
