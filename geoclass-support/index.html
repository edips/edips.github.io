Write to support

Reporting a problem

Tell us:

  • Your Geoclass version, device model and iOS/iPadOS version.
  • The steps that caused the problem and what you expected to happen.
  • Whether it happens every time, and whether the device was in portrait or landscape.

A screenshot or a small example with invented coordinates can help. Remove personal details and confidential project information. Do not send passwords, Apple verification codes, payment-card details or private survey files.

Projects and backups

Projects are stored in the app on your device. Keep an exported backup of important work in a separate location. Before deleting or reinstalling Geoclass, back up your projects; support cannot recover device-only files from a Geoclass server.

Location and online maps

If GPS does not return a position, check Geoclass location permission in iOS Settings. Location accuracy depends on your device and surroundings. Online map tiles need an internet connection. Survey coordinates and map display also depend on the coordinate reference system selected for the project.

Importing or sharing files

When using Files, iCloud Drive or another provider, make sure the chosen file has finished downloading. If a file fails to import, tell support its format and the error message. Keep the original file and avoid overwriting your only copy while troubleshooting.

Ad-Free purchases

If you bought Lifetime Ad-Free through the App Store, Geoclass checks verified ownership automatically. Use the same Apple Account that made the iOS purchase. After reinstalling, connect to the internet and open the app so StoreKit can check your ownership. If ads remain, contact support with the app version and a description of the problem.

An Android purchase does not automatically transfer to the App Store. Apple manages App Store payments and refund requests: Apple purchase support. Never send your Apple Account password to us.

Privacy and library materials

Read the iPhone and iPad privacy policy for data practices and privacy requests. Find library notices and available versioned materials on the library licences and materials page. For a materials request, include your app version and build number.
